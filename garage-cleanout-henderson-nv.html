Garage Cleanout in Henderson, NV

The garage is where everything goes to be forgotten. Old sports equipment, broken tools, boxes from three moves ago, furniture that "might come in handy someday" — it adds up. Most homeowners in Henderson and Las Vegas have a garage that could use a full reset.

Henderson Junk Solutions specializes in garage cleanouts of every scale — from clearing out one corner to a complete floor-to-ceiling haul. We carry everything out, load it up, and leave your garage clear and swept. You don't sort, you don't carry, you just tell us what to take.

We serve Henderson, Las Vegas, Green Valley, Boulder City, Enterprise, Spring Valley, and surrounding areas throughout the Las Vegas Valley.

FAQ

Garage Cleanout Questions

Pricing is based on how much volume we haul away — starting at $99 for small loads. A typical single-car garage cleanout usually falls in the quarter to half-load range. We'll give you an exact price before we start, and you're never obligated to proceed.
No sorting required. Just walk through with us when we arrive and point out anything you want to keep. We take care of everything else — no prep work on your part.
We can't take hazardous materials like paint, chemicals, motor oil, or propane. Clark County offers free household hazardous waste drop-off events — we're happy to point you in the right direction. We can haul everything else.
Most standard garage cleanouts take 1–3 hours depending on the volume and how accessible items are. We work fast and efficiently so you're not waiting around all day.
