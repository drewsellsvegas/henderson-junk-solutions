Furniture Removal in Henderson, NV Made Easy

Getting rid of old furniture in Henderson can be surprisingly difficult. Most donation centers won't pick up. Curbside pickup won't touch a sectional. And moving it yourself risks your back and your car. That's where we come in.

Henderson Junk Solutions hauls away furniture of all types and sizes — single pieces or entire rooms. We do all the heavy lifting, load everything into our trailer, and dispose of it responsibly. We donate usable items whenever possible to keep them out of the landfill.

Whether you're upgrading your living room, clearing out a spare bedroom, or moving out entirely, we've got you covered across Henderson, Las Vegas, Green Valley, and the rest of the valley.
