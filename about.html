Who We Are

Henderson's Most Trusted Junk Removal Team

Henderson Junk Solutions was founded with a simple mission: make junk removal in Henderson, NV fast, fair, and stress-free. We're a locally owned and operated business proudly serving Henderson, Las Vegas, and the entire Clark County area.

We haul everything — furniture, appliances, construction debris, yard waste, and full property cleanouts. We believe in showing up on time, giving honest upfront pricing, and leaving your property cleaner than we found it. No hidden fees, no surprises.

We're committed to keeping the Las Vegas Valley clean. Whenever possible, we donate usable items to local charities and recycle materials responsibly, keeping junk out of Nevada landfills.
