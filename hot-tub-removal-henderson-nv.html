Hot Tub Removal in Henderson, NV Done Right

Old hot tubs and spas are one of the trickiest items to get rid of. They're heavy — often 400 to 800 pounds — awkward to move, and most junk haulers won't touch them. Henderson Junk Solutions specializes in full hot tub removal, from cutting it apart to hauling every last piece off your property.

Whether your spa is in a backyard, on a deck, or tucked in a tight space, our crew has the tools and experience to get it out cleanly. We serve Henderson, Las Vegas, Green Valley, Boulder City, and the surrounding valley.
