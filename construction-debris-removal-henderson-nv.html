Construction Debris Hauling in Henderson, NV

Remodeling projects generate a lot of waste — and moving it yourself takes time and equipment most homeowners don't have. Whether you've just finished a kitchen renovation, bathroom demo, flooring replacement, or a full room gut, Henderson Junk Solutions cleans up the mess so you can focus on the finish line.

We haul all types of construction debris including drywall, lumber, tile, concrete, roofing material, flooring, and general renovation waste. We also work alongside contractors and property managers who need reliable, on-call debris removal throughout the Las Vegas Valley.

We serve Henderson, Las Vegas, Green Valley, Boulder City, Enterprise, Spring Valley, and the surrounding area.
