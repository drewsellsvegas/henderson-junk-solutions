Appliance Removal in Henderson, NV

Old appliances are heavy, awkward, and nearly impossible to move on your own — and Henderson's trash service won't pick most of them up at the curb. Henderson Junk Solutions makes it simple. We show up, disconnect (water lines and basic connections), haul the appliance away, and dispose of it responsibly.

Appliances contain refrigerants, oils, and metals that need to be handled and recycled correctly. We make sure every appliance we remove is disposed of in compliance with environmental regulations — no illegal dumping, ever.

We serve all of Henderson, Las Vegas, Green Valley, Boulder City, Enterprise, Spring Valley, and surrounding areas.
